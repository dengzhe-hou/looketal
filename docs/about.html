About

About Look et al.

A reference library of academic web pages, in the spirit of Refero Styles, for researchers and their coding agents.

What is included

100 paper project pages from papers accepted at NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR and ACL in 2025 and 2026, ten per venue and year. Candidates come from arXiv comments that state the acceptance and link a project page; a page had to load, and workshop papers were left out. That gave a pool of 505 pages, at least 40 per venue and year.

30 researcher homepages from a pool of 138: ACM Turing Award laureates (2018–2025) and first or last authors of Best Paper, Outstanding Paper and Test of Time awards at the same five venues (2023–2025) who keep a personal page. ACL Outstanding Papers are not yet in the pool. Institutional directory pages, Google Scholar and LinkedIn profiles do not count.

How pages were chosen

By visual design only, in two rounds. Every candidate was screenshotted at 1440×900. In the first round a model reviewer looked at every first screen in a pool and kept 20 (60 for researchers); in the second it looked at a 2700px-tall capture of each and kept 10 (30 for researchers). The criteria were typography, a look of its own beyond template defaults, layout and rhythm, how the work is presented, and polish. Research content, venue and fame were ignored, and near-ties went to the page that added a style not yet picked. Pages that showed a loader, a blank screen or a bot wall could not be judged and were dropped. The reason for each pick is on its style page.

How a DESIGN.md is made

From the rendered page first. A headless browser reports the computed styles of the title, headings, body, buttons and containers, the fonts that actually loaded, the first-viewport anatomy in DOM order, and the color share of the first screen. The page's stylesheet and markup then supply selectors, hover and responsive rules. When the CSS and the render disagree, the render wins. Each file follows a fixed section order (Overview, First Viewport, color, type and spacing tokens, Layout Metrics, Components, Page Modules, guidance for other content, an agent prompt guide and a Quick Start) and passes an automatic check that every color used in the prompts appears in the token table. 130 of 130 entries were checked against the rendered page.

The method was revised after a reconstruction test. Builders given only a DESIGN.md and different content rebuilt five pages, a blind judge tried to match each rebuild to its original, and an analyst scored similarity from 1 to 5 against the live page. With the first version, written from CSS alone, the judge matched all five, but overall similarity averaged 3.0 (2 to 4), mostly because of values the CSS declared and the page never showed. With the current version, the same test on the same five pages again matched all five and averaged 4.0 (all five scored 4). A separate audit of ten other files found 4 wrong values in about 129 checked claims; those were corrected.

Screenshots

Each entry shows an above-the-fold screenshot of the original page, reduced to 1200×750, credited to the authors and linked back to the live page. Screenshots are shown for reference and comparison, the same way design reference libraries such as Refero do. DESIGN.md files describe visual style; they are not official guides of the authors.

Removal

If you are an author and want your page or screenshot removed or corrected, open a removal request (a short GitHub form) and it will be taken down. Requests are handled by hand, usually within a few days.
