# d2 — Style Reference

> Nerfies template used as a lecture handout: derivations, tables and bar charts.

**Theme:** light

Measurements are normalized from static HTML/CSS (Bulma 0.9 plus the stock Academic Project Page Template `index.css`, about 1.8 KB) and roles are interpreted from selectors and one rendered look. Bulma's palette (`#00d1b2`, `#ffdd57`, `#3298dc`) appears in frequency counts but not on the page and is excluded. Several page-specific classes (`results-table`, `subfig-grid`) have no CSS rules, so they render with Bulma's reset.

## Overview

d2 keeps template chrome but is written like a short paper: eight `h2.title` sections (contributions, motivation, GRPO derivation, two estimator sections, results, conclusion, BibTeX) with `h3` sub-sections, math rendering, and a real HTML results table. The hero has a three-line Google Sans title, blue author links, "Cornell" with a small inline logo, and two dark pills (arXiv, Code); YouTube and Colab links appear later in the body. A left-aligned TL;DR paragraph (bold label, 1.6rem inline size in places) leads into a four-panel bar chart. Section headings are left-aligned `title` (Bulma 2rem), unlike the centered template default.

## Components

- **Link button:** `.button.is-normal.is-rounded.is-dark`, `#363636`, white text; arXiv and Code in the hero.
- **Affiliation with logo:** `Cornell` text plus a 30×30 floated image.
- **Contributions band:** `section.section.hero.is-light` on `#f5f5f5`.
- **Results table:** `table.results-table` with `<caption>` ("Table 1. Toxicity Score vs. FLOPs…"), two-row header with `rowspan`/`colspan`; no custom styles.
- **Sub-figure grid:** `figure.subfig` cells labeled (a)–(d) for Sudoku, Countdown, GSM8K, MATH500.
- **BibTeX:** Bulma `pre`.

## Page Modules

- **Title/author block:** five authors, single affiliation with logo.
- **Venue badge:** none on the page; ICML 2026 comes from the arXiv comment (BibTeX lists the arXiv preprint).
- **Link buttons:** arXiv, Code in the hero; YouTube and Colab links further down.
- **Teaser:** four-panel bar chart of accuracy across benchmarks.
- **Contributions:** light band.
- **Method:** Motivation, GRPO derivation for masked DLMs, d2-AnyOrder, d2-StepMerge with rendered math.
- **Results:** HTML table plus a 2×2 sub-figure grid.
- **Conclusion, BibTeX.**
- **Not present:** venue line, video tiles, comparison slider, 3D viewer, HF demo, acknowledgements.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use real HTML tables with captions for numeric results.
- Do left-align section titles when the page reads like a derivation.
- Don't leave tables unstyled; add borders and number alignment.
- Don't hard-code figure widths like 2000px; they overflow on phones.
- Don't omit the venue once the paper is accepted.
